Privacy Policy

Visual Shopping List · Effective October 18, 2026

The short version

  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your account, your lists, your pantry and the things you save.
  • No ads. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • Your household's lists, pantry and calendar are visible only to people in your household.
  • You can download everything or delete your account at any time from the Family tab.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address, from Google sign-in or the email and password you create. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see them.
  • Household information: the household name, who is in it (names and emails), shopping lists, the pantry, and the meal calendar.
  • Things you save for yourself: recipes, your own versions of items, aisle maps, and price history (what things cost at which store, and when).
  • The shared item catalog: item names, stores, aisles, prices and photos in the main catalog are seen by everyone who uses the app. Only the administrator can change them. If you send a change request, the administrator sees it with your name and email.
  • On your phone only: settings such as your last store, what you checked off lately, and saved meal ideas are kept in your browser's storage. They're removed when you sign out.

Outside services the app uses

Some features send information to these services so they can work:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check) stores your account and data and protects the app from abuse. App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA. Firebase privacy
  • OCR.space reads the text in photos you choose to scan (a handwritten list, a receipt, a price tag or an aisle sign). The photo is sent to OCR.space to be read. The app doesn't save these photos. OCR.space privacy
  • Google Gemini (through Firebase AI Logic) is used when the free text reader can't read a photo, for "photo of food", for meal ideas, and to build a recipe from a dish name. The photo or text you send goes to Google. The free version of this service may be used by Google to improve its products, so don't scan anything private. Gemini API terms
  • Open Food Facts receives a barcode number when you scan one, to look up the product name and photo. No personal information is sent. Open Food Facts privacy
  • Google Calendar opens only when you tap "Add to Google Calendar", with the meal name and time filled in for you to save.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your saved recipes, your item edits, your aisle maps and your price history.
  • Your household: lists, pantry, meal calendar and the members list. Only the household owner can invite or remove people. Invite links expire after 7 days.
  • Everyone using the app: the shared item catalog and store list.
  • The administrator: change requests you send, with your name and email.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Family tab → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Family tab → Delete my account. This removes your account, your recipes, item edits, aisle maps, price history and pending requests. If you own a household with no one else in it, that household is deleted too. Owners of a shared household must remove the other members first.
  • Leave a household: Family tab → Leave this household.
  • Skip the outside readers: you can type or paste lists instead of scanning photos.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing a household. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules make sure only signed-in, verified accounts can read data, and only the right people can see each part. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't store sensitive personal information in item names or notes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
